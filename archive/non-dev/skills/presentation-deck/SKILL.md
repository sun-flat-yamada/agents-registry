# Presentation Deck Authoring Skill

This skill guides the synthesis of professional, slide-based presentation decks for pitches, product briefs, architecture reviews, and executive updates.

## Design Architecture

### 1. Slide Structural Layouts
Decks can be authored as static structured slides or self-contained HTML/Tailwind documents:
- **Title Slide**: Eyebrow badge, bold display heading, subtitle, author/date attribution.
- **Metrics / Stats Grid**: Large key numbers (e.g. `+142%`, `$12M ARR`) with contextual micro-labels.
- **Comparison / Columns**: Side-by-side trade-off cards or feature matrix.
- **Timeline / Roadmap**: Chronological milestones with status indicators.
- **Manifesto / Quote**: High-impact, centered quote with subtle glassmorphic background styling.

### 2. Typographic & Color Hierarchy
- **Typography**: Display serif/sans for headlines; readable system sans for body and figures.
- **Color Palette**: Pick a cohesive theme (Dark Navy & Electric Blue, Warm Cream & Slate, or Minimalist Monochrome).
- Maintain consistent spacing, margins, and padding across all sequential slides.

### 3. Output Format
Save presentation files under `artifacts/presentations/<slug>/index.html` or as structured slide specs, and surface clickable preview links in chat.
